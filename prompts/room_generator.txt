Design one 2D game room as compact JSON.

Rules:
- width/height are provided by the caller.
- Use only object types: wall, platform, ladder, door, decor, enemy.
- Do not return a pixel matrix.
- Prefer rectangles and compact segments.
- Every room needs at least one 1x3 door.
- Platforms are 1 cell high.
- Ladders are exactly 1 cell wide.
- Enemies are exactly 1x1.
- Decor sizes may only be 1x1, 1x2, 2x1, 2x2, 3x3, 4x3.
- Keep the room connected and avoid overlaps.
- Return only JSON matching the schema.
